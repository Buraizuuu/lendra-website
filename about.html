About

A lending book you actually own

Lendra exists for one reason: private lenders deserve software as serious as the money they're putting at risk.

Why it exists

Most private lenders run their book out of spreadsheets, messaging apps, and paper receipts. That works until a payment gets missed, a penalty needs recalculating, or a borrower disputes what they owe, and suddenly there's no single record anyone can point to.

Lendra takes a narrower, more useful position. It's one workspace for the whole lending business: borrowers, loans, repayments, penalties, a payment calendar, and a separate tracker for capital placed outside the loan book. Every number on the dashboard traces back to the same database, so there's never a question of which spreadsheet is the real one.

And because it's local-first, that database lives on your own machine. Nobody else's server, nobody else's terms of service.

A note on risk: Lendra tracks your loans, penalties, and repayment history, but it can't make a borrower pay. Non-payment and default are real risks in private lending, and that responsibility stays with you as the lender, not with the software. Lend only what you can afford to lose.
What It's Built On

Principles the product is built around

Local-first, always

Your borrowers, loans, and payment history live in a database on your own machine. Lendra is a workspace you own, not a service you rent.

One source of truth

Dashboard totals, penalty calculations, and printed contracts all read from the same record. Nothing is reconciled by hand.

Built for one operator

No login screen, no multi-user permission model to configure. Lendra is designed around the reality of a single lender running their own book.

A real desktop app

Native window, native performance, no browser tab to leave open. Install it once and it lives on your machine like any other app.

Penalties, not guesswork

Late fees accrue automatically at a rate you set, snapshotted the moment a loan leaves overdue. No spreadsheet formula to maintain.

Nothing held hostage

Backup and restore your workspace on demand, with every uploaded document embedded. Your data is never something you have to ask permission to keep.

Being Honest

What Lendra doesn't do

Some collection apps in this space automate the borrower-facing side: reminders, nudges, even payment links. Lendra doesn't, on purpose, and you should know that going in.

No automated email, SMS, or calls

Lendra won't text, email, or call a borrower for you. Upcoming and overdue payments surface on your dashboard and calendar; following up is still on you.

No borrower-facing app or portal

Borrowers don't get a login, a link, or an app of their own. Everything is recorded by you, from your side of the desk.

No payment processing

Recording a payment as GCash or Bank Transfer just labels it. Lendra doesn't move money, generate payment links, or talk to any payment provider.

No e-signatures

Loan contracts print to PDF for a physical signature. There's no in-app signing canvas or borrower sign-off flow.

No cloud sync

Your book lives on one machine at a time. Moving between devices means an explicit backup and restore, not automatic sync.

No multi-user access

Lendra is built for a single operator. There's no team accounts, roles, or permissions to manage.
