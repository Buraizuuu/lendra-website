What's new in Lendra

Release notes and version history.

v1.0 Official Launch July 2026
  • Official launch, rebranded from an earlier internal loan tracker to Lendra, with a new visual identity: a teal "cut-corner L" mark on a warm paper neutral background.
  • Full Borrower Management: add, edit, archive, search, ID document uploads, and co-borrower (guarantor) support.
  • Loan Management with configurable amount, interest rate, payment frequency, and term across daily, weekly, semi-monthly, and monthly schedules.
  • Repayments with receipt uploads, transaction type tracking, and quick-fill for expected amounts.
  • Automatic Penalty accrual from the first missed due date, with a Lift Penalty option and full itemized breakdowns.
  • A visual Calendar with daily collections tracking and a monthly Collection Rate summary.
  • Investment tracking separate from the loan book, supporting Monthly Interest and Flat Rate contracts with a Capital Recovery progress bar.
  • Printable loan contracts and account statements with lender branding, auto-scaled to one page.
  • Multiple lending Profiles, each with isolated borrowers, loans, and settings.
  • Migrated storage to SQLite and shipped a native Tauri desktop app for Windows, macOS, and Linux.
  • Backup & restore with embedded images, Auto Backup on a configurable interval, and orphaned upload cleanup.
