Product Tour

Everything a private lender needs, in one place

From onboarding a borrower to closing out a fully repaid loan, here's the full workflow.

Dashboard

Your whole book, at a glance.

Organized by priority: what needs action today, what's operationally overdue, and where your capital actually sits.

  • Available Cash, Outstanding Balance, Total Capital, Interest Earned
  • Portfolio and Payment Methods donut charts
  • Overdue, upcoming, and pending payment alerts
Borrower Management

Every borrower, fully documented.

ID, employer, income, collateral notes, and co-borrower details, all searchable in one list.

  • ID document upload with auto file naming
  • Co-borrower (guarantor) support
  • Loan Standing rating: Good, At Risk, or Poor
Loan Management

Configure a loan exactly your way.

Amount, interest rate, payment frequency, and term, with printable contracts ready to sign.

  • Daily, weekly, semi-monthly, or monthly schedules
  • Pending, Active, Overdue, and Paid statuses
  • Auto-scaling contract and account statement PDFs
Repayments

Record it once, right the first time.

Track transaction type, attach a receipt, and get quick-fill cards for the exact expected amount.

  • Cash, GCash, Bank Transfer, Cheque, Online
  • Receipt image auto-named to loan number and date
  • Full payment history per loan
Calendar

See collections before they're late.

A visual schedule across every active loan, with actual collections alongside what's due.

  • Scheduled vs. Collected detail panel per day
  • Month summary: Collected, Remaining, Overdue, Collection Rate
Penalties

Late fees, tracked automatically.

Penalty accrues from the first missed due date at a rate you configure, with a full itemized breakdown.

  • Filter by Outstanding, Settled, or All
  • Lift Penalty to waive without collecting, fully undoable
  • Penalty clause auto-added to printed contracts
Investments

Track capital beyond your loan book.

Money placed in external businesses or individuals, with two contract types and a Capital Recovery progress bar.

  • Monthly Interest or Flat Rate contracts
  • Partial or full capital returns, dated notes log
  • Settle and reopen investments anytime

Why Lendra

Built for how private lenders actually work

Local-First, Always

Your book lives in a local SQLite database on your own machine. No cloud dependency, no third party sees your ledger.

Full Penalty Automation

Late fees accrue automatically at your configured daily rate, snapshotted the moment a loan leaves overdue.

Print-Ready Documents

Loan contracts and account statements with your branding, auto-scaled to one page, ready to sign and file.

A Real Desktop App

Native window, native performance, no browser tab to leave open. Install it once and it lives on your machine like any other app.

Multiple Lending Books

Separate profiles for separate lending books, each with isolated borrowers, loans, and settings. Switch instantly.

Backup and Restore

Export a full snapshot on demand or automate it on an interval, with every uploaded image embedded in the backup.
