See Lendra in action

Every core workspace, large and in full, from onboarding a borrower to closing out a loan.

01 · Dashboard

Dashboard

Organized by priority: action required, operational overview, financial summary, and analytics.

  • Available Cash, Outstanding Balance, Total Capital, Interest Earned
  • Portfolio by Amount & Payment Methods donut charts
02 · Borrower Management

Borrowers

Add, edit, archive, and search borrowers with full ID and co-borrower details.

  • ID document image upload
  • Co-borrower (guarantor) support
03 · Loan Management

Loans

Configurable amount, interest rate, payment frequency, and term, with printable contracts.

  • Daily, weekly, semi-monthly, monthly schedules
  • Pending, Active, Overdue, Paid statuses
04 · Repayments

Repayments

Record payments with transaction type and receipt image, plus quick-fill for the expected amount.

  • Cash, GCash, Bank Transfer, Cheque, Online
  • Full payment history per loan
05 · Calendar

Calendar

Visual payment schedule across all active loans with daily collections tracking.

  • Scheduled vs. Collected detail panel
  • Month summary with Collection Rate
06 · Penalties

Penalties

Late payment penalties across every loan, filterable by Outstanding, Settled, or All.

  • Per-loan itemized penalty breakdown
  • Lift Penalty, waive without collecting; fully undoable
07 · Investments

Investments

Capital placed in external businesses or individuals, separate from your loan borrowers.

  • Monthly Interest or Flat Rate contracts
  • Capital Recovery progress bar
08 · Settings

Settings

Per-profile lender info, currency, logo, signature, contract terms, and Auto Backup, all in one place.

  • Light / dark theme, 15 supported currencies
  • Backup & restore, orphaned upload cleanup
